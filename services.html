Cupping Therapies & Treatments

Eleven cupping methods and the health concerns people most often ask us about. Tell us what is going on and we will tell you honestly whether cupping is a sensible option.

Our Cupping Therapies

Which one is right for you depends on your health and what you want addressed. We will recommend it after a short consultation.

Therapy image

Hijama (Wet / Blood Cupping Therapy)

The traditional form of hijama. Cups are applied with a light vacuum and a small amount of blood is drawn, following the classic method.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Fire Cupping Therapy

A quick flame creates the vacuum in each cup before it is placed. Sessions are short and the sensation is strong.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Hijama Without Head Shave

Wet hijama carried out on the areas you choose, so you can keep your hair. Suits people who prefer not to shave.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Cupping Massage Therapy

Slow gliding strokes with the cups over the skin, closer to a deep massage than a static treatment. Often used for stiffness.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Dry Cupping Therapy

Suction only, with no blood drawn. A gentler option that many people book to help with tight, tired muscles.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Bamboo Cupping Therapy

Traditional bamboo cups sealed with heat or suction instead of plastic or glass. The older option, and one people ask for by name.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Horn Cupping Therapy

Suction created with a natural horn rather than a plastic cup. The pull is gentler and the rim sits differently on the skin.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Rotating Cupping Therapy

The cup moves around the area under suction instead of sitting in one place, giving broader and more even coverage.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Silicone Cupping Therapy

Soft silicone cups that flex with the skin. Comfortable on sensitive areas and good for longer, slower sessions.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Magnetic Cupping Therapy

Cups fitted with magnets are moved over the area under suction. Some people find the pull easier to sit through.

Book on Call
Therapy image

Weight Loss Therapies

Cupping alongside sensible guidance on movement, food and sleep. Supportive care, not a replacement for medical weight management.
