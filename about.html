About Al Shifa Cupping Therapy

Al Hijama is a traditional practice carried across the Middle East and South Asia for a long time. At Al Shifa Cupping Therapy in Hyderabad we work within that tradition, but we hold it to modern standards of hygiene and patient care.

Every session begins with a short consultation. We ask about your health, anything you are taking, and what you would like to address, then explain which approach seems most appropriate and why. If cupping is not the right route for you, we will say so rather than sell you a session. If you are weighing up which is the best cupping therapist in Hyderabad to trust, we would put hygiene and honesty at the very top of that checklist – and we build every session around the two.

Hygiene is the part we take most seriously. Instruments are sterilised before every appointment, gloves and dressings are single-use, and the treatment room is cleaned and aired between clients. We would rather turn someone away than compromise on any of that.
